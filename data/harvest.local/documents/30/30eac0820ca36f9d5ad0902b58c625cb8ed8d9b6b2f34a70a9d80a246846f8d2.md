## Pay

- **Pay Levels 1, 4**: “The BDes curriculum has a four-level structure leading to a gradual movement from general, foundational courses at level 1 to more specialized inputs at level 4” (p. 11)
- **Pay Levels 1, 2**: “As the student moves upwards from level 1 to level 2, the student graduates to choosing between courses which offer different kinds of skills from soft to hard” (p. 11)
- **Pay Level 1**: “The courses at level 1 would help the students to choose the mix of skills they prefer to acquire” (p. 11)
- **Pay Level 3**: “At level 3, it is hoped that the student is ready to fine tune a certain mix or a set of skills into a specialization” (p. 11)
- **Pay Level 4**: “At Level 4, the student employs all that has been gained regarding skills and understanding and applies or uses them in independent projects” (p. 11)
