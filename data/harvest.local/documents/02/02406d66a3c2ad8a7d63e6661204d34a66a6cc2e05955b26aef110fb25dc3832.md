- “Candidates, for being eligible for admission to Ph.D, must possess the minimum eligibility criteria in terms of – (i) educational qualification and, (ii) relevant NET/JRF/GATE subjects, as prescribed by the respective School/Centres/Special Centres of the University duly mentioned against each of…” (p. 2)
- “Candidates having only educational qualification but not having qualified NET /JRF/GATE in the relevant subjects or vice versa, shall not be eligible for admission” (p. 2)
- “Candidates desirous of admission in these programmes of the university should satisfy themselves about their eligibility criteria prescribed for admission as mentioned against Ph.D Programmes of the concerned School/Centre/Special Centres and availability of seats therein as per intake given in…” (p. 3)
- “They must fulfill the eligibility criteria both in terms of educational qualification and relevant NET/JRF subjects failing which they shall not be considered for admission to Ph.D programme.” (p. 3)
- “Only those candidates are eligible for admission to Ph.D programmes through JRF who fulfill the minimum eligibility criteria as prescribed by the respective School/Centre/Special Centre mentioned in the e-Prospectus against each of them and who have qualified for” (p. 3)
- “Provided that a candidate seeking admission after a 4-year/8-semester bachelor’s degree programme should have a minimum of 75% marks in aggregate or its equivalent grade on a point scale wherever the grading system is followed” (p. 8)
- “A candidate, for being eligible for admission to Ph.D Programme, must possess the prescribed Educational Qualification and UGC/CSIR NET percentile of June 2025 onwards of NET Examinations of UGC/CSIR in the Relevant/Allied NET/JRF Subject/s as mentioned against each Ph.D Programme below” (p. 15)
- “Mere possessing the educational qualification and not possessing NET percentile of the Relevant/Allied NET/JRF Subject/s of June 2025 onwards of NET examinations of UGC/CSIR and vice versa shall make a candidate ineligible” (p. 15)
