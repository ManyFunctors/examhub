## Eligibility

Education levels named: Graduate

- “EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATIONS:” (p. 3)
- “a) Must have passed Bachelor's Degree of any University in India established or incorporated by or under a Central Act, State Act or Provincial Act or an Institution recognized by the University Grants Commission or any equivalent qualification.” (p. 3)
- “b) Must have passed Telangana Government Technical Examination in English Typewriting by Higher Grade (45 words per minute), or equivalent examination” (p. 3)
- “C) Must have knowledge or qualification in computer operation.” (p. 3)
- “e) The candidate shall possess the aforesaid Educational Qualifications as on the date of this notification” (p. 3)
- “a) As on 01-07-2026, all the candidates (except the ex-service person), must have completed the age of 18 years and must not have completed the age of 46 years.” (p. 3)
- “To apply under this category, the aforesaid candidates must have put in at least two years of service by the date of publication of this notification..” (p. 4)
- “In case of a tie in age also, the candidate with highest marks in the educational qualifications prescribed would be considered.” (p. 6)
