## Eligibility

Education levels named: Doctorate, Graduate, Diploma / ITI, Class 12

- “EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION:” (p. 4)
- “Candidates should possess the following or its equivalent qualification awarded by a University or Institution recognised by the University Grants Commission for the purpose of its grants” (p. 4)
- “The courses must have been approved by the Medical Council of India” (p. 4)
- “Must have served as House Surgeon (CRRI) for a period of not less than twelve months” (p. 4)
- “Candidates should have registered their names in the Tamil Nadu Medical Council on or before the date of this notification” (p. 5)
- “If a candidate claims that the educational qualification possessed by him/her is equivalent though not the same as those prescribed for the appointment, the onus of proof rests with the candidate” (p. 5)
- “Candidate on the date of the Board’s Notification for the post should possess adequate knowledge in Tamil” (p. 5)
