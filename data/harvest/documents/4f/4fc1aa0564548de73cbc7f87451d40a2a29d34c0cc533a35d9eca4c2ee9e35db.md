## Eligibility

Education levels named: Postgraduate, Graduate

- “(a) A candidate must have attained the age of 21 years and must not have attained the age of 32 years on the first day of the month of January of the year in which the Examination is to be held [i.e. as on 1st January, 2027] i.e. he/she must have been born not earlier than 2nd January, 1995 and…” (p. 9)
- “N.B.: Candidates should ensure that they meet the age eligibility criteria for each post mentioned above for which they are applying” (p. 9)
- “(III) Minimum Educational Qualifications :” (p. 12)
- “(i) For Geologist Group ‘A’ in Geological Survey of India Master’s degree in Geological Science or Geology or Applied Geology or Geo- Exploration or Mineral Exploration or Engineering Geology or Marine Geology or Earth Science and Resource Management or Oceanography and Coastal Areas Studies or…” (p. 12)
- “Master’s degree in Geology or applied Geology or Marine Geology or Hydrogeology from a University incorporated by an Act of the Central or State Legislature in India or other educational Institutes established by an Act of Parliament or declared to be deemed as Universities under Section 3 of the…” (p. 12)
- “(vi) For Assistant Geologist Group ‘B’ in Geological Survey of India Master’s degree or Integrated Master’s degree in Geology or Applied Geology or Mineral Exploration or Marine Geology or Earth Science from a recognized University or Institute” (p. 12)
- “NOTE II: Master's degree in respective discipline means the post graduate degree of minimum two years duration after graduation from aforesaid University or Institute in the respective discipline, whether be it an integrated course or otherwise” (p. 13)
- “The Commission shall provide a window of 10 days after the declaration of result of Main/Stage-II part of Combined Geo-Scientist Examination to the candidates qualified for Personality Test/Interview and who have filled their status of educational qualification” (p. 13)
