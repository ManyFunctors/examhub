## Eligibility

Education levels named: Graduate

- “INSTRUCTIONS TO BE READ CAREFULLY The requisite Educational qualification, Age, Physical standards, Procedure to be followed for selection, etc., are given below” (p. 15)
- “i) Age Limit (General): Must have attained the age of 21 years and must not have attained the age of 25 years as on 1st July, 2026” (p. 16)
- “Educational Qualification:” (p. 17)
- “i) Must possess a Degree awarded by any university in India, established or incorporated by or under a Central Act, Provincial Act of State, or of any Institution recognized by the University Grants Commission or any other equivalent qualification as on the date of Notification, i.e., 29th July 2026” (p. 17)
- “Unless such Degrees had been recognized by the relevant Statutory Authority, they will not be accepted for the purpose of Educational Qualification” (p. 18)
- “Technical Qualification for Post Code 17: Candidates must have possessed a valid Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) driving license as on the date of Notification” (p. 18)
- “ii) Each eye must have a full field of vision” (p. 18)
- “iv) Candidate should possess sound health and be free from any bodily defect or infirmity which will render him / her unfit for the Posts notified” (p. 19)
