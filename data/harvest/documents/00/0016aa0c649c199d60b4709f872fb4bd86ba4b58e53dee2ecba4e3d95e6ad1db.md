## Eligibility

Education levels named: Graduate

- “AGE & EDUCATIONAL QUALIFICATION:” (p. 3)
- “(For fulfillment of eligibility criteria relating to age, educational qualification, etc.)” (p. 3)
- “Minimum 20 years and Maximum 28 years as on cut-off date i.e. candidates must have been born not later than 01.11.2006 and not earlier than 02.11.1998 (both days inclusive)” (p. 3)
- “Educational Qualification” (p. 3)
- “Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized University or any equivalent qualifications recognized as such by the Central Government” (p. 3)
- “Candidates must have passed their Graduation on or after 01.04.2022 and” (p. 3)
- “The candidate should possess a valid OBC certificate with a non-creamy layer clause as per the Government of India guidelines, from time to time” (p. 4)
- “ Both the candidate as well as scribe will have to give a suitable undertaking confirming that the scribe fulfils all the stipulated eligibility criteria for a scribe mentioned above” (p. 6)
