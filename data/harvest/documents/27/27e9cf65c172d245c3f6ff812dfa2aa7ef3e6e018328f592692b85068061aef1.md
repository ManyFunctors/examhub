## Eligibility

Education levels named: Graduate

- “(i) The candidates must have passed the BDS Examination from any recognised Dental College of Bihar State included in the schedule of Dental Council of India” (p. 4)
- “(iii) The candidates must have completed the rotating Internship on or before 31.05.2026 as per the provision contained in the Information bulletin of NEET(MDS)-2026” (p. 4)
- “(iv) The candidates must have obtained Registration from Bihar State Dental Council or Dental Council of India” (p. 4)
- “A candidate who is not a permanent resident of Bihar but has passed the BDS Examination from any Dental Colleges of Bihar State included in the Schedule of Dental Council of India, is also eligible” (p. 4)
- “* For Eligibility Criteria and other details, required Documents for admission on (DQ) Quota, candidates are advised to see 'Chapter 4: Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD) of Information Bulleting & Counselling Scheme of NEET-MDS Counselling 2026' which can be seen on MCC Official Website” (p. 9)
- “All the certificates included in the list given below, if applicable, must have to upload at the time of downloading his/ her Provisional Seat Allotment Order and have to submit in original by every candidate at the time of Document Verification & Admission process” (p. 11)
- “In addition to this, the individual should have a speech discrimination score of more than 60%” (p. 23)
