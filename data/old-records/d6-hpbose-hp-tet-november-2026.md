The Board's own instructions page was read directly and gives the application dates, the fee and the
late-fee window recorded above; a four-day correction window runs from 9 October 2026 to 12 October
2026, and after that no offline or online request for correction is entertained, and corrections
cannot be made in the category or sub-category field. The prospectus announces the examination across
a series of dates from 22 November to 6 December 2026, and the Board conducts one test for several
subjects and levels on those dates, so no single examination date is recorded. The Board's earlier
June 2026 cycle, which ran from 7 to 21 June 2026, is a completed cycle and is not recorded here.
