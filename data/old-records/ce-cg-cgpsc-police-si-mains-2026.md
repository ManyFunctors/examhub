The main examination runs over three consecutive days, 2, 3 and 4 November 2026. The commission
moved it out of the 24 to 26 October 2026 window because of a clash with the teacher recruitment
examination. Three consecutive days have no single exam date, so the field is left empty rather than
reduced to a first or last day.
