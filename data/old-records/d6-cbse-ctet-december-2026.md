The notice of 14 September 2026 announces the test on two days, 12 and 13 December 2026, so no
single examination date is recorded here. The notice gives dates only; it does not restate the mode
of examination, the fee or the eligibility, and none of those is recorded. No application window
for this cycle had been published on the CTET portal when this record was checked.

The notice describes this as the 22nd edition, but the board's notice of 8 May 2026 for the test
held on 6 September 2026 also described that test as the 22nd edition. The edition number in the
two notices therefore does not agree, and no edition number is carried in the title. The
September 2026 cycle is a completed cycle and is not recorded separately.
