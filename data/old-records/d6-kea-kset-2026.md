The KEA portal was checked directly and carries the KSET-2026 entry and the application link, and the
notification for the test was issued on 10 August 2026. The notification reproduced the schedule as
a provisional timetable: submission of the online application from 10 August 2026, last date for
the application 5 September 2026, last date for the fee 6 September 2026, hall ticket 1 October 2026
and the examination on 11 October 2026. The whole test runs in one session on one day, so a single
examination date is recorded. The notification is silent on negative marking, so nothing is stated
about it.
