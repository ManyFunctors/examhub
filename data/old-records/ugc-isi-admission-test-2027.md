summary = '''The ISI Admission Test is the single written test through which the Indian Statistical
Institute selects students for its degree programmes, including the Bachelor of Statistics
(Hons) and the Bachelor of Mathematics (Hons), the two-year Master of Statistics and Master of
Mathematics, the two-year Master of Science programmes in quantitative economics, library and
information science and quality management science, the two-year Master of Technology in
Computer Science, and the Junior Research Fellowship in Quantitative Economics, offered across
ISI Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi and Hyderabad. Candidates who clear the written test are
shortlisted for interview for the programmes that run one, and the ranking for those programmes
is built from the written score and the interview together. The test is a pen-and-paper
examination held on a single Sunday at centres in several cities. ISI announces the date of the
following test in the preceding autumn, having published the date of the 2025 test on 8 October
2024, and the 2026 test was held on 10 May 2026. The notice for the 2027 test has not been
published, so no date is recorded.'''

eligibility = '''Eligibility is programme-specific and set out in the ISI prospectus. Several programmes,
including the two-year Master of Technology in Computer Science, require Mathematics at the
higher secondary level, while the two-year Master of Statistics and the two-year Master of
Science in Quantitative Economics require a bachelor degree of three or more years duration in
any discipline. Candidates in the final year of the qualifying degree may apply, provided the
final examinations are completed before the cut-off date fixed by the Institute for the
programme.'''
