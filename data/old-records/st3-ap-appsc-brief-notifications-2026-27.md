The Commission split the twenty notifications into four application windows, so the closing date
stored above is the last of them. Applications open from 6 October 2026 and close at 11:59 p.m. on
27 October 2026 for notifications 07, 08, 11 and 19 of 2026; from 9 October 2026 to 29 October
2026 for notifications 25 and 26; from 13 October 2026 to 2 November 2026 for notifications 09,
10, 14, 20, 21 and 22; and from 16 October 2026 to 5 November 2026 for notifications 12, 13, 15,
16, 17, 18, 23 and 24. No examination date has been published for any of these services, so none
is recorded. Group-II and Group-IV Services advertisements had not been issued by the Commission
when this record was checked.
