Online applications run from 21 August 2026 to 18 September 2026, with the fee payable to 19
September 2026, so the window closes in under three weeks from the date this record was written. The
Authority has not fixed the examination date, so none is recorded. The pay scale recorded above is the
2024 revised State Pay Scale published with this notification. This is the recruitment, not the
eligibility test: the Karnataka State Eligibility Test is a separate record. A separate Karnataka
Examinations Authority recruitment for the Karnataka Residential Educational Institutions Societies
(KREIS), for 1,087 teacher posts, closed on 17 August 2026 and is a completed cycle, not recorded
here.
