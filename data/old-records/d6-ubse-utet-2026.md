The Board published the notification on 14 July 2026 and opened registration the same day. The
original last date for the application was 3 August 2026 with the fee to 6 August 2026 and a
correction window from 8 to 11 August 2026; the Board then extended the last date for submitting the
application to 9 August 2026, with the fee to 11 August 2026, and the extended date is the one
recorded above. The Board has since published the admit card, so the test is imminent. The test is
held on a single day, 29 September 2026, so a single examination date is recorded. The mode of
examination is given differently in different reports and the notification was not read on this
point, so no mode is recorded. The separate Uttarakhand State Combined B.Ed. Entrance Test is an
admission test for the B.Ed. course, not an eligibility test, and is not recorded here.
