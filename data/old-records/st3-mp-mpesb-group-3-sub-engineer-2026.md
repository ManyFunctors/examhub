The Board replaced the first rule book of 17 August 2026 with a second one on 27 August 2026. The
figures stored here are from the second rule book: the application window of 29 August to
12 September 2026, a correction window running to 17 September 2026 and the written examination
from 07 October 2026. The earlier rule book had set 01 September 2026 as the closing date and
06 October 2026 as the start of the written test, and a number of reports still carry those. The
examination runs over more than one day, so no single examination date is recorded. Biometric
verification with Aadhaar at the centre is mandatory, and an e-Aadhaar is accepted only if it is
UIDAI-verified.
