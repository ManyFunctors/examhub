No dates at all on this record. As at 27 September 2026 the Commission has published
no notice of examination for this cycle. The official tentative calendar lists the
advertisement month as September 2026, the closing month as October 2026 and the
examination as January to March 2027, all of which are month-level only. The
examination runs over four or more weekly windows with a preference system, so the
exam_date field must stay empty even once those windows are published. Rifleman
(GD) in the Assam Rifles is recruited through this same notice, not separately.
