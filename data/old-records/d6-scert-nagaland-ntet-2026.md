Both papers are held in one day, on 21 November 2026, so a single examination date is recorded. The
SCERT page says it is awaiting the guidelines and directions of the National Council for Teacher
Education on the eligibility, syllabus and modalities for a Secondary Level TET for classes 9 to 12,
and that action will be initiated on receipt, so no secondary-level test is announced for Nagaland.
The fee is tabulated in the Guidelines and Syllabus document and was not transcribed, so no fee is
recorded. This is the sixth edition; SCERT has conducted the test in 2013, 2016, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
