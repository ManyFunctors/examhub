The dedicated TG SET portal at tset.telangana.gov.in did not respond when it was checked on
27 September 2026, so the university own domain is recorded here as the official site and the
dedicated portal should be reached from the university website. The notification was released in July
2026 and registration ran from 3 August 2026 to 30 August 2026 without late fee, with escalating
late-fee windows running to 12 September 2026. The examination is run as a computer based test from
28 to 30 September 2026, across three days, so no single examination date is recorded. The
notification is silent on negative marking, so nothing is stated about it. The Telangana State Teacher
Eligibility Test for the June session, held 16 to 22 June 2026, and the Telangana TGTET for in-service
teachers, held on 7 and 11 September 2026, are separate tests and are not recorded here.
