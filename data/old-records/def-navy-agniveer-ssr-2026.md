No exam date. The Navy published the online application for Agniveer (SSR),
Agniveer (MR) and SSR (Medical) for INET 2026 from 14-03-2026 to 06-04-2026 and has
since issued Stage-II call-up letters, but no single Stage-I examination date is
published on the portal for this cycle and the selection spans several stages and
centres, so the field is omitted. Stage-II call-up letters for the 01/2027 batch
were issued in September 2026. The application correction window was 10-04-2026 to
11-04-2026.
