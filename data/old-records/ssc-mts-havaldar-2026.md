No dates at all on this record. As at 27 September 2026 the Commission has published
no notice of examination for this cycle. The official tentative calendar lists the
advertisement month as June 2026, the closing month as July 2026 and the examination
as September to November 2026, all of which are month-level only, so none of them is
recorded as a date. The exam date is the normal trap here: the computer based test
historically runs over many consecutive days, so even once a window is published the
exam_date field should stay empty.
