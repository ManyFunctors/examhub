Each of the seven notifications states that the exact dates for submission of the Stage-I online
application form, and the dates of the preliminary written test, the physical tests and the trade
or driving tests, will be notified separately through a press release published on the board's
website. None of those dates had been published when this record was checked, so no dates are
recorded here. The payments toward the application are made only through the online mode on the
board's website.
