The Driver (2024) cycle is live but is not run as a single examination. The board has taken the
recruitment through at least six published phases of the Vocational Trade Test, the latest schedule
issued on 16 September 2026, and the recruitment reaches document verification after the trade test.
Each phase has its own dated press note on the board's news page, and the schedule PDF for the
current phase is what a candidate holding a call letter needs. The trade test dates could not be read
from the schedule document, so no date is recorded here. The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board
advertisement for Driver (2024) carries the eligibility, the licence and experience requirement and
the pay scale.
