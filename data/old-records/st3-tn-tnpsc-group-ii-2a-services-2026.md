The preliminary examination is a screening stage only and its marks are not carried into the final
merit list. In the Group II main examination, Paper I is qualifying and only the marks in Paper II
count for ranking, on the basis of which the rank list is published and candidates are called to
certificate verification and counselling in the ratios the notification sets out, 1:3 and 1:2 for
onscreen certificate verification in the General and reserved categories respectively. The date of
the main examination had not been fixed when this record was checked.
