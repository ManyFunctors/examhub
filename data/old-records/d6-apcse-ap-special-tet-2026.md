Registration opened on 25 August 2026 and closes on 24 September 2026, so the window is open on the
date this record was written. The examination runs from 11 October to 21 October 2026, across
eleven days, so no single examination date is recorded. The initial answer key is published on 24
October 2026, objections are accepted from 25 October to 1 November 2026, the final key on 10
November 2026 and the result on 15 November 2026. The notification is silent on negative marking, so
nothing is stated about it. The regular APTET June 2026 cycle, under Notification No. 01 dated 5
June 2026, was held from 5 to 21 August 2026 with the result declared on 15 September 2026; it is a
completed cycle and is not recorded here.
