summary = '''GPAT is the national entrance examination for admission to the two-year Master of Pharmacy
course and for the award of scholarships for M.Pharm and Ph.D. courses. The National Board of
Examinations in Medical Sciences conducts it on behalf of the Pharmacy Council of India and
hands the result to the Council; NBEMS has no role in counselling or in the disbursement of
scholarships, and the institutions admit candidates on the basis of the score after verifying
their documents and eligibility themselves. The test is a computer-based multiple choice paper
run once a year at centres across the country. GPAT 2026 was held on 7 March 2026 and the
result was declared by 7 April 2026; NBEMS has not yet published the information bulletin or
opened the application portal for the next cycle, so no date is recorded here. The eligibility,
paper structure and fee are those published in the GPAT 2026 information bulletin.'''

eligibility = '''Indian citizen holding a bachelor degree in pharmacy, four years after 10+2 and including
lateral entry students, or an equivalent qualification from a recognised university. Candidates
appearing for the final year, that is the fourth year, of the Bachelor of Pharmacy examination
whose result will be declared before the commencement of admission to the M.Pharm course for
the academic year are eligible, and so are candidates appearing for the pre-final, that is the
third, year whose fourth-year result will be declared before the commencement of M.Pharm
admission for the following academic year. B.E. or B.Tech. degree holders in Pharmaceutical and
Fine Chemical Technology, or equivalent, are not eligible. Appearance in GPAT confers no
automatic right of admission to an M.Pharm course or of the scholarship.'''
