The computer-based test was run over a window rather than a single day, from 01.07.2026 to 30.07.2026
across both cadres, with admit cards live from 23 June 2026. No single exam date is recorded, because
picking the first or last day of the window would tell a candidate nothing about their own shift. The
physical screening and physical measurement tests follow the written examination, and the department
has not published a single consolidated date for them either.
