The advertisement fixed the preliminary examination tentatively on 1 November 2026. The
Commission's second corrigendum, dated 22 September 2026, moved it to 22 November 2026 because the
Odisha Civil Services Main (Written) Examination 2025 was scheduled over the same period, so the
revised date is the one stored above. The preliminary marks are not counted in the final merit
list, which is built on 600 marks of main written examination and 50 marks of personality test.
