summary = '''AIEEA (PG) is the entrance examination for the two-year master's degree programmes in
agricultural and allied sciences at the agricultural universities accredited by the Indian
Council of Agricultural Research. The National Testing Agency conducts the examination on behalf
of ICAR, which then uses the NTA score for the ICAR share of seats in those programmes and for
the ICAR postgraduate scholarship, holding the counselling itself; the individual university
applies its own admission conditions. The examination is run in the same session as the AICE
JRF/SRF examination for doctoral research degrees, as a separate paper, and in the 2026-27
session both were held on 4 July 2026. No notification has been published for the next cycle,
so no date is recorded. Note also that for undergraduate programmes in agriculture and allied
sciences ICAR has moved to the NTA-run Common University Entrance Test, CUET (ICAR-UG), and
ICAR issued an information bulletin for the CUET-based 2026 intake rather than a separate
undergraduate examination of its own.'''
