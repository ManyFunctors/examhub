summary = '''Jamia Millia Islamia runs its own entrance examination for a large part of its undergraduate
and postgraduate programme structure, and admission to a number of its other programmes is made
on the strength of national-level scores instead. The University does not publish one single
test: the prospectus carries an annexure that lists, for each programme, a separate entrance
test date, reporting time, and the date by which the selected-candidate list is displayed, and
some programmes add a written test component, an interview, a group discussion or a practical
test after the entrance test. Because the examination is therefore spread across many programmes
and many dates, it is not possible to record a single examination date for the cycle. Nothing
has been published for the 2027-28 session; the most recent cycle, 2026-27, ran on the
programme-wise schedule set out in Annexure I of the University Prospectus 2026-27, with tests
between April and June 2026, and the prospectus for the coming session is normally issued in
the months before the applications open.'''
