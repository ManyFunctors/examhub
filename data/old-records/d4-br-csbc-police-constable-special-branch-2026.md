What makes this recruitment different from the rest of the constable drives is that there is no
Physical Efficiency Test at all, no running and no jumping, and no interview. Selection is the written
examination followed by the Physical Measurement Test only. The written examination is two papers of
90 minutes each, 100 questions and 100 marks per paper, one mark for each correct answer and 0.25
marks deducted for each wrong answer. A candidate scoring below 30 per cent in the written examination
does not proceed to the physical measurement stage, and the final merit list is drawn on the marks in
the main written examination alone.

The Physical Measurement Test is a measurement, not a performance, and the standards are: men of the
Unreserved and Backward Class categories 165 cm, men of the Extremely Backward Class, Scheduled Caste
and Scheduled Tribe categories 160 cm, and women of all categories 155 cm. Chest measurement applies
to male candidates only. A minimum weight of 48 kg is prescribed for female candidates.'''
