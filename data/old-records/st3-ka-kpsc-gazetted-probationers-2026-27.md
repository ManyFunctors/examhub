One Time Registration on the KPSC portal is compulsory before the application can be filed. The
notification carries 15 November 2026 as the date of the preliminary examination and labels it
tentative, so it is stored as a provisional date; the Commission states that the exact date, time
and venue will be intimated separately. The main examination and personality test dates are not in
the notification and will be announced later.
