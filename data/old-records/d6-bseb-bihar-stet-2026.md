The Board has not announced the date of the examination, so no examination date is recorded. The
Board own STET site still showed the 2025 cycle, with the 2025 result, admit card and objection
links, when it was checked on 27 September 2026, so the 2026 notice has to be reached through the
Bihar Vidyalaya Pariksha Samiti homepage. The notification offers no correction window, so the form
is final on submission, and applications submitted after the last date, or submitted without the
fee, are rejected. The notification is silent on negative marking, so nothing is stated about it. The
Bihar Public Service Commission TRE-4.0 recruitment is a separate advertisement and is recorded
separately.
