The test is run in a single session on a single day, so a single examination date is recorded. The
portal runs the application in two steps: fee collection from 22 June 2026, extended to midnight of
25 July 2026, and online registration from 22 June 2026, extended to midnight of 26 July 2026, which
is the deadline recorded above. The information bulletin gives the eligible subject list, the marks
in Paper I and Paper II and the qualifying requirement in each paper. The portal is silent on
negative marking, so nothing is stated about it.
