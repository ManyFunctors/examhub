summary = 'Aptitude test prescribed by the Council of Architecture, under the Council of Architecture (Minimum Standards of Architectural Education) Regulations, for admission to the first year of the five-year B.Arch degree at recognised institutions. The Council is both the exam body and the prescribing authority; there is no centralised council-level counselling, and each institute and the states run their own admission process. Under the regulations the aptitude test may be either NATA or the JEE (Main) B.Arch paper conducted by NTA. The test runs in two phases, the first on designated Fridays and Saturdays over a long window, the second a single two-day window for candidates who did not sit Phase 1.'
