Registration was first announced to open on 21 September 2026 and was then rescheduled to open on
25 September 2026, so the window is open on the date this record was written, and it closes on
26 October 2026 with the fee payable by the same date. The Commission said it would announce the
complete TRE-4.0 examination schedule later, so no examination date is recorded; earlier press
figures for the examination window of 22 to 27 September 2026 belong to the superseded schedule.
Earlier press figures for the size of the recruitment, 46,595 and 46,882 posts, were superseded by
the 32,388 posts in the current advertisement. No pay scale is recorded: the post-wise scale is in
the advertisement and the figure circulating in the press could not be tied to a published scale.
This is the recruitment, not the qualifying test. The qualifying test for it is the Bihar STET or
CTET, which is a separate advertisement and is recorded separately.
