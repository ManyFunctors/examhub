Both papers are held in one day, on 11 October 2026, so a single examination date is recorded. The
application window closed before this date notification, and the Directorate has moved on to verifying
candidature, so no registration dates are recorded. An earlier notification of the same series dated
15 September 2026 records the provisional acceptance and rejection of applications and the district
preference requirement. The notification is silent on negative marking, so nothing is stated about it.
