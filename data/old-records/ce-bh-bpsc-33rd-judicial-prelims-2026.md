The commission has postponed this examination in compliance with an order of the Hon'ble Supreme
Court of India in Writ Petition (Civil) No. 1110 of 2025, and has separately published an
examination programme for the same advertisement. The commission's 2026 examination calendar had
carried 30 May 2026 for this examination; that date lapsed with the postponement and no revised
date has been announced, so no date field is filled.
