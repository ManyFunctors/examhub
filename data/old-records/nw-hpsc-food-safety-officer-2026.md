Science, Veterinary Sciences, Bio-Chemistry, Microbiology, Chemistry or medicine, or a
medical degree such as MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, BUMS or an equivalent qualification notified
by the Food Authority with the prior approval of the Central Government, from a recognised
university. The training specified by the Food Authority is to be completed after
appointment, and the person appointed must have no financial interest in the manufacture,
import or sale of any article of food. Hindi or Sanskrit must have been studied at
Matriculation, or Hindi at the higher secondary level. Age 18 to 42 years as on 19 October
2026, the closing date of the online application, with relaxation of 5 years for Scheduled
Castes and Backward Classes of Haryana, persons covered by the Rights of Persons with
Disabilities Act 2016 (to a maximum of 52 years for Group A and B posts), wives of military
personnel disabled while in service, widowed, divorced, judicially separated and unmarried
women. Haryana domicile is a hard gate: the benefit of reservation will be given only to
those DSC, OSC, BC-A, BC-B, EWS and PwBD candidates who are residents or domiciles of
Haryana, and BC-A and BC-B candidates who fall in the creamy layer as per Haryana Government
notification No. 40/13/2024-I dated 16 July 2024 are not eligible to claim it and must apply
under the General category. The post is at Pay Scale 9300-34800+4200 GP, FPL-6 (Cell-I). The
45 posts are distributed as Unreserved 22, DSC 5, OSC 5, BC-A 5, BC-B 3 and EWS 5, plus 2
ex-servicemen (General) and 2 PwBD posts.'''
