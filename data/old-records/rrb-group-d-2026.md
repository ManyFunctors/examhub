No exam date and no closing date for applications. The revised tentative CBT
schedule ran the computer based test in four separate blocks between 03-08-2026 and
25-08-2026 in three shifts a day, which is a set of dates rather than a single day.
The CEN states that dates for PET, Document Verification, Medical Examination and
Empanelment will be intimated from time to time through the websites of the Railway
Recruitment Cells, and none had been published as at 27 September 2026. The CEN as
printed on 30-01-2026 gives an application closing date of 02-03-2026, but Corrigendum
No. 4 of 02-03-2026 revised the notified dates and the revised deadline was not
readable, so registration_deadline is left out rather than guessed. The indicative
notice in Employment News was 27-12-2025.
