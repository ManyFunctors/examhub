No exam date. The notice states that dates for the CBT, Document Verification and
Medical Examination will be communicated from time to time through the official websites
of the concerned RRBs, and none had been published as at 27 September 2026. The
indicative notice in Employment News was 25-07-2026; the fee payment deadline was
15-09-2026; the modification window is 16-09-2026 to 25-09-2026 and the scribe
details window is 26-09-2026 to 30-09-2026. Corrigendum 1 added further posts after
publication.
