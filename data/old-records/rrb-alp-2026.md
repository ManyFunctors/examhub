No exam date. The Boards' own FAQ states that information on the examination
schedule and venue will be given to eligible candidates in due course through the
RRB websites, SMS and email, and no CBT-1 schedule notice had been published as at
27 September 2026. The indicative notice in Employment News was 21-03-2026; the
application correction window was 17-06-2026 to 26-06-2026. Candidates apply to one
RRB only.
