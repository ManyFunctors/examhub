No exam date. The Army published the common entrance examination schedule
category by category, not as a single date: Agniveer (General Duty) and Agniveer
Tradesmen (10th) on 01 and 02 June 2026, 03 and 04 June 2026, 05 and 08 June 2026
and 09, 10 and 12 June 2026, each with its own admit-card release date four days
earlier. No single exam_date is recorded. The online written test is over and the
Phase II recruitment rallies are being held by the individual Army Recruiting
Offices, which publish their own dates; the next cycle's common entrance examination
notification had not been issued as at 27 September 2026.
