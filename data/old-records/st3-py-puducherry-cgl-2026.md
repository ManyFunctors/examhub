The Puducherry Examining Authority runs recruitment for all the departments of the Union Territory
through the recruitment portal run by the National Informatics Centre. It has uploaded a tentative
schedule for the competitive written examinations filling vacancies in various departments, but has
not published a fixed date for this examination, so no exam date is recorded. A separate
notification announcing the date of the competitive examination for selection to Home Guard, male
and female, is live on the same portal.
