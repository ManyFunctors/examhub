The application is in two steps: one-time registration and then the form, with the last date for
depositing the application fee on 4 October 2026 and the last date for correction in the online
application on 8 October 2026. The written examination is on a single day, 3 November 2026, so a
single examination date is recorded. The advertisement is silent on negative marking, so nothing is
stated about it. The regular UPTET 2026 under Advertisement No. 01/2026 was held on 2, 3 and 4 July
2026 and the result was declared on 26 August 2026; it is a completed cycle and is not recorded here.
