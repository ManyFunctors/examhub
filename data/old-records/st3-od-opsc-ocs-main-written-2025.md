The written examination runs over five days, so no single examination date is recorded. Paper I,
Odia, and Paper II, English, are on 29 October 2026; the Essay on 30 October 2026; General Studies
Papers I and II on 2 November 2026; General Studies Papers III and IV on 4 November 2026; and the
two optional subject papers on 7 November 2026. The admission certificate and instructions to
candidates are downloadable from the Commission's website in the week before the examination.
