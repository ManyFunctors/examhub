notification fixing the examination date and its step-by-step application instructions are both
scanned image files. The dates transcribed above were legible in those files and are corroborated by
the Board own press release, which is a separate document, and by independent reporting, so they
are recorded. Nothing else has been read out of the scans. The test is held in one sitting on one
day, 30 September 2026, so a single examination date is recorded; that date falls three days after
this record was written. The Odisha Teacher Eligibility Test for primary and upper primary classes
was held on 28 June 2026 and is a separate, completed cycle, not recorded here.
