No exam date. The Boards have issued a tentative schedule putting the computer
based test for Technician Grade I and Grade III from 06-10-2026 to 09-10-2026. That is a
four-day window rather than a single day, so no exam_date is recorded. The CEN
itself says the dates for the CBT, Document Verification and Medical Examination will
be communicated from time to time through the official websites of the RRBs. Fee
and post-wise age limits are left out because they were not verified against the
notice. The application modification window was 01-08-2026 to 10-08-2026 and the
scribe details window was 11-08-2026 to 15-08-2026.
