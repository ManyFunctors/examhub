The single written examination for all three cadres was held on 20 September 2026, with the state
cadre paper in the morning and the Kalyana Karnataka paper in the afternoon, and separate mark
sheets for each post category. Because that sitting has already taken place and the Commission had
published no date for the Physical Standards Test, the Physical Efficiency Test or the medical
examination when this record was checked, no examination date is recorded here.
