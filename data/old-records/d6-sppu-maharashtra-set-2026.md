The 41st M-SET has been postponed three times. The university first advertised it for 26 July 2026
and admit cards had been issued for that date; the test did not take place then. The university
published a corrigendum and a new prospectus and the portal now announces the examination for
25 October 2026, which is a single day, so a single examination date is recorded. No registration
dates are recorded: the July application window had closed before the postponement, the university
has not reopened a window for the October date, and the application and late-fee dates reported in
the press for the July cycle conflict with one another.
