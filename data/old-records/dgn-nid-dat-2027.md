summary = '''The Design Aptitude Test is the entrance examination of the National Institute of Design
for admission to the Bachelor of Design, the five-and-a-half-year integrated Master of Design
and the two-and-a-half-year Master of Design programmes at NID Ahmedabad with its extension
campuses at Gandhinagar and Bengaluru, and at NID Andhra Pradesh, NID Haryana, NID Madhya
Pradesh and NID Assam. Admission runs in two stages. The DAT Prelims is a paper-and-pencil
test written on the same day at every test centre; its score is used only to shortlist
candidates and carries no weight in the final ranking. Selection rests on the DAT Mains, which
comprises a Studio Sensitivity Test and an In-Person Sensitivity Test, the latter covering the
portfolio and an interview, and each NID uses the Mains score for admission to its own
campus. A single application covers both the undergraduate and the postgraduate pathway,
because the entrance examinations for the two levels are held simultaneously on the same day.
The Admissions Handbook 2027-28, published on 10 September 2026, fixes the dates recorded
below and marks every DAT Mains date, as well as the weightages for the final result, as to
be announced later.'''

eligibility = '''Applications for the academic year 2027-28 are open to candidates who have passed or will
appear for the higher secondary (10+2) qualifying examination in the academic year 2026-27 in
any stream, from a board of education recognised by the Association of Indian Universities, such
as CBSE, IB or ICSE, or an equivalent examination listed in the handbook. Age limit for the
undergraduate and integrated pathways: candidates born on or after 1 July 2006 in the General,
Gen-EWS and PwD categories, and on or after 1 July 2003 in the OBC-NCL, SC and ST categories.
Master of Design applicants must hold a bachelor degree in any discipline and be born on or
after 1 July 1995 in the General and Gen-EWS categories, or on or after 1 July 1992 in the
OBC-NCL, SC and ST categories. A backlog, compartment or supplementary paper in the qualifying
examination, or a Class XII result not submitted by 15 July 2027, renders admission null and
void.'''
