ExamHub is a reference for upcoming Indian exams: their dates, what you need to
qualify, and a link to the official notification each entry was built from.

It covers India only, and only exams still ahead. Nothing is archived. Once an
exam has run it comes off the site, rather than gathering past entries that
nobody is looking for.

It is not a preparation portal. There is no study material here, no
recommendations and no coaching.

Bodies reschedule, sometimes at short notice, so the notification is the
authority on any given date. Read it before you act on one.

Built with Hugo. No database. No tracking.
