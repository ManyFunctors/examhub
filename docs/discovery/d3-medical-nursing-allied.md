# Discovery: medical, nursing and allied-health recruitment (d3)

Research date: 2026-09-27. Method: open-ended `websearch` in student vocabulary
("staff nurse govt exam", "medical officer recruitment", "AIIMS NORCET", "ESIC",
"GPAT", "paramedical government jobs after BPT"), then breadcrumb-following from each
national test to the institutions that consume it, then verification against the body's
own domain.

**Scope note.** NEET, NEET-PG, NEET-SS, NEET-MDS and the other admission and entrance
exams are already covered in this repository, and so are AIIMS NORCET-11, AIIMS CRE-5 and
the ESIC Nursing Officer cycle drawn from NORCET-11 (`alh-*` records). This pass covered
**recruitment only** and weighted nursing and allied health, which remains the thinnest
area in the repository.

### The decisive gate: council registration

Across every nursing notice read in this pass, the line that actually disqualifies people
is the council registration, not the degree. Three variations worth recording verbatim,
because applicants misread them:

- NORCET and CRE: "registered as a nurse and midwife with the State or Indian Nursing
  Council", and the *closing date of the application* is the crucial date — registration
  must be complete by then, not by the exam.
- PGIMER: "registered as a Nurse or Nurse and mid-wife (RN or RN & RM) with **State**
  Nursing Council" — State council specifically.
- State bodies: MMSRB requires registration with the **Meghalaya** Nursing Council;
  TN NHM requires the **Tamil Nadu** Nurses and Midwives Council *obtained before the
  notification date*; NHM Assam requires the **Assam** Nurses', Midwives' and Health
  Visitors' Council.

The experience requirement interacts with registration: for GNM candidates the two years
(or one year) must be counted only after the course residency period, after declaration
of result, and after registration.

## 2. National tests, who consumes them, and where each body's own notice lives

| National test | Conducted by | Consumed by | Where the *consuming body's* recruitment notice lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NORCET** (Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test) | AIIMS New Delhi Examination Section, `aiimsexams.ac.in` | 27 participating AIIMS and allied central hospitals: AIIMS New Delhi, Bathinda, Bhopal, Bhubaneswar, Bilaspur, Deoghar, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Kalyani, Mangalagiri, Nagpur, Patna, Raebareli, Rewari, Rishikesh, Vijaypur, Avantipora, AIIMS-CAPFIMS, and ESIC (523 of 2,218 posts in NORCET-11), JIPMER Puducherry, Dr RML Hospital, Safdarjung Hospital, NITRD | AIIMS examination section publishes the single test for all; ESIC publishes its own recruitment entry at `esic.gov.in/recruitments`; AIIMS Rishikesh and AIIMS Raipur also mirror the advertisement PDF on their own institute sites, which is useful when `aiimsexams.ac.in` is slow |
| **CRE** (Common Recruitment Examination, CRE-5) | AIIMS New Delhi | 32 AIIMS and central government institutes/bodies; posts grouped so one fee covers several similar institutes | `aiimsexams.ac.in`; individual participants (CNCI at `cnci.ac.in`, AIIMS Kalyani at `aiimskalyani.edu.in`) mirror the DRA PDF, which is often faster to reach than the AIIMS site |
| **CMS / CMSE** (Combined Medical Services Examination) | UPSC | Central Health Service, Railways (ADMO), NDMC, Municipal Corporation of Delhi; and **ESIC Insurance Medical Officer Grade-II**, which is filled only from UPSC's Pratibha Setu list of the *previous* year's CMS candidates | UPSC runs the exam; ESIC publishes a separate short notice at `esic.gov.in` for IMO Gr. II, addressed to candidates already in the CMS merit list. This two-step route is easy to miss and worth a page of its own |
| **RRB Paramedical CEN** | Railway Recruitment Boards | Indian Railways zonal railways and production units | `rrb.indianrailways.gov.in` for the detailed CEN, `rrbapply.gov.in` for the application, and the participating regional RRB sites |
| **UPSC Recruitment by Selection** | UPSC | Individual ministries — DGAFMS (Civilian Medical Officer), Ministry of Ayush (Medical Officer/Research Officer, Unani), MoHFW, Ministry of Culture (Scientist 'B'), Govt. of Puducherry | `upsc.gov.in`; application through the UPSC ORA portal at `upsconline.nic.in/ora/` |

**The fragmented mapping that is worth building out:** from any national test, the
consuming body publishes *its own* recruitment rules and its own eligibility, even though
the test is shared. NORCET's single advertisement carries AIIMS's 18–30 age band and
2-year GNM experience rule, but ESIC's entry carries its own upper age of 30 with a
5-year relaxation for its own employees, and ESIC's own one-year GNM experience rule.
Two institutions, one test, two different eligibility texts. The same is true of CRE-5
across its 32 participants, and of CMS across the four employing services.

## 4. Deliberately omitted: multi-day examinations

The `exam_date` field was **omitted** from two records where the retrieved notice text
indicated more than one sitting:

- **RUHS MODRE-2026.** The Information Booklet's Important Dates table is a two-column
  layout. The offline OMR examination row reads `13-12-2026 (Sunday)` and the adjacent
  cell reads `14-12-2026 and 15-12-2026`, which is almost certainly a three-day
  examination written in the Hindi half of the booklet as well. A single-day `exam_date`
  would have been a guess. Needs a human read of the PDF at `ruhsraj.org`.
- **RRB Paramedical CEN 05/2026.** The CBT date is not published at all; the previous
  cycle's CBT ran over three days in March 2026. Omitted until RRB publishes a notice.

The same trap was anticipated elsewhere: SGPGIMS Lucknow ran its Nursing Officer Stage-I
over 22–25 June 2026 and its Stage-II is still "to be notified separately".

## 7. Two systematic gaps, stated plainly

**`fee` is absent from all nine records.** Every fee figure I could find — PGIMER's
₹1,500/₹800/nil, MMSRB's ₹460/₹230, HPPSC's ₹600/₹150, JKSSB's ₹600/₅00 — appeared only
in aggregator summaries of the notices, never in the notice text I was able to read
directly. The repository's own rule is that aggregator values are discovery-only and are
never stored as fact, so the field is omitted rather than filled from a source that has
been caught contradicting notices before. A person with the PDFs can fill nine fee fields
in about twenty minutes. This is the single highest-value follow-up on this batch.

**`negative_marking` appears on one record only.** HPPSC's own advertisement states that
the Paper-I screening test carries negative marking, but the per-question deduction was
not legible, so the field records the existence of negative marking and says where the
figure is. For PGIMER, RRB, RUHS, JKSSB, MMSRB and the TN NHM drive, no official statement
of the rate was read and the field is omitted.

## 8. What to chase next

1. **`fee` on all nine records**, from the PDFs.
2. **RUHS MODRE-2026 examination window** — confirm whether it is 13 December alone or
   13–15 December, then add `exam_date` accordingly.
3. **RRB Paramedical CBT date** — it will be notified separately after the application
   closes, and RRB paramedical CBTs habitually run over several days, so expect to omit
   `exam_date` even when the notice lands.
4. **The AIIMS rolling-advertisement pattern.** AIIMS Jammu runs cycle-based Senior
   Resident rolling advertisements; AIIMS Rishikesh, Raipur, Patna, Jodhpur, Bhopal and
   others run their own periodic ones. A single "AIIMS senior resident rolling
   recruitment" page listing each institute's current cycle and cut-off date would be more
   useful than twelve near-identical records.
5. **The ESIC Pratibha Setu route.** ESIC Insurance Medical Officer Grade-II is filled
   from the *previous* year's UPSC CMS merit list through a separate ESIC notice. That is a
   two-body pipeline that no exam portal presents as one route.
6. **State health society notices are almost all offline or walk-in**, and several are
   DIPR-display advertisements with no stable URL. TN NHM is offline with a hard-copy
   deadline; Ri-Bhoi is a district office notice; Chhattisgarh NHM posts are walk-in. These
   will keep falling through the cracks of any portal that assumes an online form.
7. **Pharmacist is a thin, high-value category.** UPSC Drug Inspector (Level 8) and the
   state and university Pharmacy Officer drives (BFUHS Punjab, JKSSB, HPRCA, KEA) are the
   main doors, and UPSSSC had 1,002 posts approved in Uttar Pradesh with no notification
   issued as of this research date — worth watching.
