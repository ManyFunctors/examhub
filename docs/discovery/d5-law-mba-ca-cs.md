# Discovery note — law, MBA, CA, CS and commerce graduates

**Checked 2026-09-27.** Thirteen records written, all `content/exams/d5-*.md`.

The persona who searches "CLAT after graduation" and "exams for CS qualified" has
a problem no aggregator publishes a solution to. They do not have a list of exams
they are allowed to sit. They have a *credential*, and nobody has drawn the map from
credential to the doors that credential unlocks. A CA is eligible for things a BCom
graduate is not, and the only places that mapping exists are the individual
advertisements, each written for a different post.

So this note is the map, not a list. The map comes first; the records are the
evidence.

A CA is **not** eligible for UPSC Combined Medical Services. The CMS is an MBBS
entry test; a commerce or law graduate is screened out at the eligibility stage.
Searching "UPSC CMS eligibility for chartered accountants" returns a confident
answer that does not exist. I did not open a live CMS notification this session —
the conclusion is that the exam is out of scope for this persona, not that a
particular year's notification says so.

| Opens | Gate | Verified how |
|---|---|---|

| **CAG audit posts** | "Company Secretary" named in the desirable qualification for the same CAG posts. | ssc.gov.in notice PDF. |

The CSEET is the one place where the map is actually published, and the shape of
it is worth stating because it is unusual: **a graduate does not need CSEET if they
already hold ICAI Intermediate, ICMAI Final, a bachelor's degree, a postgraduate
degree, or are in the sixth semester of a five-year integrated programme.** CSEET
is the route for everyone else, and for the June 2026 session onward it has been
restructured with a fresh syllabus.

## The breadcrumb nobody follows: a qualifying exam has *tiers*, and they are exclusive

This is the single most mis-stated thing in how these exams get written about, and
it applies to at least two of them.

**UGC NET / CSIR-UGC NET awards exactly one of three things, per subject.** NTA's
own public notice for the June 2026 cycle states the test determines eligibility
for: *"award of Junior Research Fellowship and appointment as Assistant Professor"*,
*"appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to Ph.D."*, or *"admission to
Ph.D. only"*. Which one you get is decided by where your score falls against a
**per-subject cut-off** — not by your overall percentage, and not uniformly across
subjects. A candidate can clear the lectureship bar in one subject and not in
another. The JRF tier additionally carries an upper age limit, reckoned at the time
of joining; the Assistant Professor route has none. *(The 30-year JRF ceiling is
the long-standing UGC provision — the December 2026 information bulletin is where
the exact reckoning date gets fixed, and it is not out.)*

**CAT has no single admitting institution.** IIM Indore convenes the 2026 test.
It does not admit you to anything. Each of the 21 IIMs applies its own shortlisting
criteria to the same score, and many other institutions use it under their own
terms. The IIMs are explicit in the notification that they "have no role in the
selection process of non-IIM institutions". Anyone writing "IIM Indore will admit
you" has misread the convening arrangement.

**CSIR-UGC is about to become a three-body exam.** From the December 2026 cycle
the CSIR-NET (Life Sciences) paper and the DBT-BET (Biotechnology) paper merge
into a single Joint CSIR–UGC–DBT JRF-NET examination. CSIR HRDG put this out on
14 January 2026. It is not a commerce or law qualification either way — but it is
the kind of structural change that silently invalidates a two-year-old study plan.

## Dead ends, traps and things that look live but are not

**The IBBI Grade A date trap.** Searching IBBI Grade A 2026 returns
`ibpsreg.ibps.in` showing "Recruitment of Officer Grade 'A' (Assistant Manager)
2026" with registration 03/09/2026 – 24/09/2026. **Those dates are PFRDA's, not
IBBI's.** The same dates appear on the PFRDA vacancy page for its own Grade A
advertisement issued 3 September 2026. IBBI's own site shows no live Grade A
recruitment in 2026 — only deputation-based postings (Manager on deputation,
18 August 2026; extension of the last date for Executive Director on deputation,
21 September 2026; Whole Time Member, 31 August 2026). Do not file an IBBI record
off those dates.

**SEBI Grade A has no 2026-27 cycle yet.** The 2025-26 cycle is complete — Phase 1
on 10 January 2026, Phase 2 on 21 February 2026, final result 23 June 2026. Every
"SEBI Grade A exam date 2026" page currently in search results is prediction
writing, typically "expected December 2026 – January 2027". No notification has
been issued, so no record.

**Other regulator cycles that are not live:** RBI Grade B (2026 advertised in
April–May, window closed — and rbi.org.in search results are dominated by 2007
and 2008 archives, which is a trap in itself); IRDAI Grade A (nothing live found);
NFRA ("no active announcements"). The one regulator Grade A that *is* live is
PFRDA, and it is already in the repository.

**IIBF CAIIB December 2026 has postponed its own registration.** IIBF's notice of
1 September 2026 states the CAIIB/CAIIB Electives registration "was scheduled to
commence from 01-09-2026. However, due to technical reasons, the registration has
been postponed. The revised date for commencement of registration will be
communicated shortly." With no announced date there is nothing to store, so no
record. The exam window itself (6, 12, 13, 20, 27 December 2026) is paper-wise and
would be omitted under the multi-date rule regardless.

**UGC NET and CSIR-UGC NET December 2026 are calendar-only.** NTA's published
examination calendar lists UGC NET (December Cycle) 14–19 December 2026 and Joint
CSIR UGC NET (December Cycle) 20–21 December 2026 — but under the column heading
**"Proposed Date(s)"**, with the application form not opening until around the third
week of November 2026. A proposed calendar and a multi-day window are two separate
reasons not to store a date. Both are covered in this note instead; neither is a
record. UGC NET is the one that matters for this persona, since it carries Commerce
and Law subjects, and its three exclusive benefit tiers are described above.

**NLU Delhi runs a second, undiscovered entrance.** The AILET 2027 admission
announcement also announces an **AIET 2027 on 2 May 2027** for: the Postgraduate
Insolvency Programme (two years); MA / L.L.M. in Insolvency Law and Restructuring
(one year); a Joint Master's / L.L.M. in Intellectual Property Law and Management
(one year); L.L.M. in Digital Technology, Policy & Governance (one year); and
Ph.D. The detailed notification is due January 2027 with forms from the second week
of January 2027. An insolvency LL.M. is exactly what a CA, CS or corporate lawyer
should be searching for, it is run by the University rather than by CLAT, and it is
not in any CLAT syllabus listing. No dates stored yet.

**ACCA is changing underneath people.** June 2027 is the final session for the
Applied Skills and Strategic Professional examinations; the redesigned qualification
phases in from 2027. A candidate starting now is on the last run of the current
structure.
