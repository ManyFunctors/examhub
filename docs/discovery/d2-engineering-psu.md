# Discovery note — engineering PSU and public-sector technical recruitment

Research date: 2026-09-27. Method: open-ended `websearch` from a final-year
engineering student's likely queries, then verification on each body's own
notice board and, where reachable, the advertisement PDF itself.

## The headline finding

The GATE → PSU trail has turned over. In late August and September 2026 the
GATE-consuming PSUs were publishing **GATE 2027** short notices, advertising
posts for an exam that will not be sat until February 2027 and whose results
arrive in March 2027. The GATE 2026 cohort was, by September 2026, largely spent
— the big 2026 intakes had closed between February and April 2026. A student
searching "PSU recruitment through GATE 2026" in September 2026 is a year behind
the actual advertising cycle.

The single most confusing consequence: **the GATE year accepted is not the
calendar year of the advertisement.** A notice published in September 2026 may
demand GATE 2027, GATE 2025, or (as one outlier does) GATE 2026 — and each PSU
draws the line differently.

### Bodies that have abandoned GATE for their own CBT — do not search for a GATE notice

- **ONGC** — Engineer and Geologist Graduate Trainee, E1, Advt 2/2026 (R&P).
  Recruitment is through ONGC's own four-section Computer Based Test
  (Concerned Subject, General Awareness, Aptitude, English), 2 hours, 52 posts.
  No GATE score required or accepted for that cycle.
- **SAIL** — the GATE route has been replaced by a direct CBT for Management
  Trainee (Technical) in E1 grade: 124 posts, applications 15 Nov – 5 Dec 2025,
  CBT 23 Feb 2026, then GD and PI. A shortlisting percentile rule applies
  (50th percentile for UR/EWS, 40th for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD).
- **BHEL** — Engineer Trainee recruitment now runs through a BHEL Computer Based
  Examination (CBE) plus interview, not GATE. The 2025 cycle ran 1–28 Feb 2025
  for applications with the CBE on 11–13 Apr 2025.
- **IOCL** — the 470-post 2026 intake (14 Aug – 3 Sep 2026) was a
  computer-based test plus GD/GT and interview, not a GATE shortlist.
- **Coal India** — Management Trainee via CBT, already recorded in this repo.

## Which branches each PSU actually takes

This is the part that is genuinely scattered and is worth more than any cutoff
table.

- **Electrical (EE) is the broadest single draw.** POWERGRID, GRID-INDIA, NTPC,
  GAIL, NSPCL, NML, NLCIL, MRPL, THDC and NTPC Mining all recruit on EE.
- **Electronics (EC) is next.** POWERGRID, NTPC, NSPCL, NML, NLCIL, THDC.
- **Mechanical (ME)** — GAIL, NSPCL, NML, NLCIL, MRPL, THDC, NTPC.
- **Civil (CE)** — POWERGRID, NTPC, NML, NLCIL, THDC.
- **Computer Science (CS)** is newly prominent in the 2027 cycle: POWERGRID,
  GRID-INDIA, NTPC, NML, NLCIL, THDC. GRID-INDIA, the successor to POSOCO, is
  built around EE and CS only.
- **Instrumentation (IN)** — GAIL, NSPCL, THDC, and the Hydro/thermal names.
- **Mining (MN)** is unusually well served for a niche branch: NTPC, NTPC Mining,
  NLCIL, THDC.
- **Chemistry (CY)**, **Environmental Science (ES)** and **Geology and
  Geophysics (GG)** appear only at THDC in this cycle, and **Chemical
  Engineering (CH)** only at GAIL and MRPL. BARC's OCES intake and NPCIL are the
  other places these branches surface.
- **Petroleum / Applied Petroleum, Production, Metallurgy, Agriculture-related
  and Mining-adjacent** streams are visible at ONGC, BARC, SAIL and NLCIL but
  have largely disappeared from the GATE-consuming power-sector PSUs.

## The GATE validity trap, stated plainly

For PSU recruitment a GATE score is generally valid for **one recruitment
season only**, unlike the three-year validity that matters for M.Tech and Ph.D.
admission. The advertisements say this in almost identical words. Examples, all
verbatim from the notices:

- POWERGRID: "only GATE 2027 score (Normalized Marks out of 100) is valid for
  this recruitment process. GATE score of 2026 or prior is not valid."
- GRID-INDIA: "only GATE-2027 score (Normalized Marks out of 100) is valid for
  this recruitment process. GATE score of 2026 or prior is not valid."
- GAIL: "only GATE‐2027 marks are valid for this recruitment exercise. GATE
  marks of 2026 or prior to that is not valid."
- NSPCL and NML: "GATE marks of previous years or any other examination shall not
  be accepted."

So the honest planning advice is: sit the GATE of the year whose results the
PSU you want will consume, and do not assume last year's score carries over.
NLCIL is the counter-example that proves the rule is not universal — it was
still recruiting on **GATE 2025** in September 2026.

## Cut-offs: deliberately not stored

No GATE cut-off score has been written into any of these records. Cut-offs are
set per PSU, per discipline, per category and per shortlisting ratio, and change
with the difficulty of the GATE paper and the number of vacancies. Several bodies
do not publish a number at all and instead shortlist on a percentile or on
normalised marks within a ratio:

- GRID-INDIA shortlists category-wise on normalised GATE 2027 marks out of 100,
  weightage 85 per cent, GD 3 per cent, PI 12 per cent, and states that
  management may raise the eligibility bar or hold a screening test to limit how
  many are called.
- BARC states plainly that the cut-off for screening into interviews "will be
  finalized only after the conclusion of the online examination" and advises
  candidates to use **both** screening channels.
- SAIL, in its CBT cycle, used a 50th percentile for UR/EWS and 40th for
  SC/ST/OBC/PwBD rather than a marks cut-off.

The correct record of a GATE dependency is "GATE is required, in this paper, for
this year" — which is what the `eligibility` prose in each record carries.

## What a future pass should check next

1. **BARC OCES/DGFS-2027.** Watch barc.gov.in and barc.edu.in from about
   December 2026. It is the single largest DAE intake and it is the only body
   that takes three GATE years at once.
2. **ONGC and IOCL have both abandoned the GATE shortlist** for their own CBTs.
   If the trail "GATE score → PSU offer" is the student's mental model, ONGC and
   IOCL are no longer on that map, and SAIL and BHEL have left it too. Re-check
   whether they return to GATE for the 2027 season.
3. **The detailed notifications that are still missing vacancies:** POWERGRID
   (March/April 2027), GAIL (February 2027), NTPC, NSPCL, NML, THDC, NLCIL. Every
   one of them has published only a short notice so far, and every one of them
   will publish the real dates only after its examination results.
4. **GRID-INDIA** is a name a student will not have heard of, and it is the
   former POSOCO — worth an alias, since "POSOCO recruitment" still dominates
   search results.
